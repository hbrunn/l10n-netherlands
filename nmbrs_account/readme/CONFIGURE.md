If your general and analytic account codes match those of Nmbrs, you don't need to do anything.

If they differ, fill in the code Nmbrs uses in the `Nmbrs` tab on the account form, or in the `Nmbrs Code` field on analytic accounts.

You can match multiple Nmbrs accounts to one Odoo account by filling in several codes in the `Nmbrs Code`, separated by spaces.
